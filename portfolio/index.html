I am a Machine Learning Engineer with a DPhil in Engineering Science from the University of Oxford, working at the intersection of scientific machine learning, numerical modelling, and AI for engineering. My work spans materials, structural and civil engineering, dynamics and modal analysis, and electrical / semiconductor applications. I am especially interested in simulation-driven ML, inverse problems, physics-informed models, and tools that turn engineering data into structured knowledge.

Projects

  • SimContext (Hugging Face Space)
    An AI-assisted engineering tool that turns ANSYS and Abaqus simulation input files into structured FEM reviews and LLM-ready context. It extracts engineering metadata, identifies modelling risks, and helps make legacy simulation models easier to understand, reuse, and feed into downstream AI workflows. Try it here.
  • MINT
    Masonry in-situ testing and material identification. Funded by the Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council (EPSRC), the MINT project estimates mechanical properties of existing masonry assets using Digital Image Correlation (DIC) and the Virtual Fields Method (VFM). Learn more.
  • High-speed camera based operational modal analysis
    Comparison of camera-based Operational Modal Analysis (OMA) with conventional EMA using piezoelectric sensors. A Python toolbox, pyOMA, was developed implementing the Frequency Domain Decomposition (FDD) method and validated on a reference structure, with focus on sensor weight effects.
  • AI at BMW MINI Plant Oxford
    In collaboration with the Oxford SDG Impact Lab, we worked with the BMW MINI Plant Oxford to create a roadmap for deploying AI tools in manufacturing, improving efficiency and sustainability. Learn more.
